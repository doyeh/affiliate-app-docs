Privacy Policy

Affiliate Auto Publisher  ·  Last updated: May 2026

Summary: This App does not collect, store, or share any personal data from users other than the App owner. It is a private, single-user tool.

1. Information We Collect

Affiliate Auto Publisher is a private application used exclusively by its owner. The App does not collect personal information from any third-party users. The only data processed by the App includes:

  • TikTok account credentials of the App owner (stored locally and securely)
  • Video content created by the App owner
  • Performance analytics from the owner's TikTok account

2. How We Use Information

All data accessed through the TikTok API is used solely to:

  • Publish video content on the owner's TikTok account
  • Retrieve performance analytics for the owner's content
  • Schedule posts for optimal engagement times

3. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on the App owner's private server. No data is transmitted to or stored on any third-party servers beyond TikTok's own platform.

4. Third-Party Services

This App integrates with the TikTok API. Please refer to TikTok's Privacy Policy at tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy for information on how TikTok handles data.

5. Data Retention

Analytics and scheduling data are retained locally for up to 90 days and then automatically deleted. TikTok access tokens are stored securely and refreshed as needed.

6. No Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer any information to third parties.

7. Contact

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the App owner directly.
